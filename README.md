## About

I'm a frontend developer who has worked on live production websites during my internships, building responsive, polished interfaces with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

I'm currently a **Shopify Developer Trainee**, focused on **theme development**: Online Store 2.0, Liquid, sections and blocks, and custom storefront experiences. I've also worked with page builders such as **Replo** and **Instant**, which gave me a practical view of both the no-code and custom-code sides of Shopify.

My goal is simple: build fast, clean, well-crafted websites that look great and are easy to maintain.

<br />

## Currently

- Building Shopify themes with Liquid and Online Store 2.0
- Working with Shopify CLI, metafields and metaobjects
- Refining animation and interaction details with GSAP
